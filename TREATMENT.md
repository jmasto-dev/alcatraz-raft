# Treatment: *Fifty Raincoats* (The 1962 Alcatraz Escape Raft)

**Style:** Scratchboard Long Take (`printed-long-take`)  
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~48.0 s  
**Language:** English narration + burned-in English subtitles + `subtitles_en.srt`  
**Color Palette:** Ink `#16171b`, Paper `#ede5cd`, One Red `#c4332b` (the raft's rubber intake valve & its vanishing ripple), Warm Yellow `#dfa443` (prison steam-lamp / searchlight beam only)

---

## 1. Three Candidate Structures (and Choice)

1. **Blueprint-to-Water Cross-Section:** Start on a flat architectural diagram of the cellblock, then peel sheets away like an engineering manual until the raft is assembled on a table and launched. *Weakness:* Feels too static and diagram-heavy; misses the physical tension of a single unbroken camera move through 3D space.
2. **Countdown of Parts (1 to 50):** Count up from raincoat 1 to 50 in a locked frame, then cut to the bay. *Weakness:* Repetitive locked frames underuse the medium's continuous tracking camera and depth parallax.
3. **One Unbroken Descent — Workshop to Bay (CHOSEN):** Begin high above the dark, wind-cut San Francisco Bay and Alcatraz Island at night, descend into the upper cellblock workshop where stolen raincoats are stitched and vulcanized over hot steam pipes, pass **through the steam** (native move) as the raft inflates via the converted concertina bellows, and glide out past the seawall following the tiny red valve of the raft as it disappears into the ink-black bay.  
   - **Why chosen:** It uses the style's signature unbroken 3D tracking shot, pairs three distinct spatial scales (island wide → workshop close-up → bay surface low glide), and places the medium's native **Through the Steam** transition right at the engineering climax (vulcanization & inflation).
   - **Opening image:** High wide of Alcatraz Island's silhouette against a wind-cut night sky, a single warm-yellow searchlight sweeping the ink water, and a plate of type cut into the sky.
   - **Ending:** Low glide behind the 6×14 ft raft as it slips into the fog; the camera rises and pulls back as the single red rubber valve—and its faint red ripple—vanishes into the ink-cut bay.
   - **Score shape:** Taut pizzicato bass and dry woodblock/knife ticks during the assembly; a sudden **silence** before the concertina bellows draws its first breath; a slow, low horn and bowed vibraphone pulse as the raft hits the water, fading to wind and bay swell.

---

## 2. Logline and Arc

- **Logline:** Across months of secret nights above Alcatraz's cellblock, three inmates engineer a seaworthy 6×14-foot raft from fifty stolen rubber raincoats, steam-vulcanized seams, and a converted concertina bellows—then vanish into San Francisco Bay.
- **Setup (0–14 s):** Establish Alcatraz at night, descend into the clandestine upper-tier workshop where 50+ stolen rubber raincoats are gathered and cut.
- **Turn (14–31 s):** The engineering core—seams are glued and pressed over hot prison steam pipes (passing **through the steam**), and a musical concertina is rigged as an air bellows to inflate the 6×14 ft hull.
- **Ending (31–48 s):** Wooden paddles bite the water; the raft launches past the jagged seawall into San Francisco Bay, its single red intake valve shrinking into the ink waves until only the dark water and sky remain.

---

## 3. Benchmark

- **Reference works:**
  1. *The Great Escape* (1963) — methodical, tactile focus on improvised prison engineering (bellows, scavenged materials, silent night work).
  2. *Lynd Ward's wood-engraved novels* — stark ink-and-paper contrast, dramatic diagonal knife cuts for night light and water, zero wasted detail.
- **What to learn:** Let tools, seams, and air pressure carry the drama; use high-contrast silhouettes and tight framing on mechanical actions (pressing a seam, pumping a bellows).
- **What not to take:** Character melodrama, faces/dialogue, or static page-by-page cuts—keep everything inside one continuous 3D camera move.

## 5. Beat Sheet (Second by Second)

- **0.0–3.0 s:** Hook—high wide over ink bay; warm-yellow searchlight sweeps; sky plate `FIFTY RAINCOATS` carves left-to-right.
- **3.0–7.5 s:** Narration 1 begins at 1.2 s ("June 1962. High above Alcatraz's cellblock, three inmates engineered an escape out of raincoats."). Camera sinks toward the roof vents.
- **7.5–11.5 s:** Camera passes through the vent bars into the dim workshop; stacks of rubberized coats and shears appear.
- **11.5–15.0 s:** Narration 2 ("Over months, they stole more than fifty rubberized coats, cutting and stitching them into a six-by-fourteen-foot hull.") Board/stencil `50+ COATS` held in frame.
- **15.0–20.5 s:** Narration 3 ("To seal the seams watertight, they pressed the rubber against hot prison steam pipes, vulcanizing the joints.") Camera tracks the pipe; steam wisps build.
- **20.5–23.0 s:** **Silence 1** (music drops out, only hiss of steam). Camera pushes into the steam cloud.
- **23.0–24.0 s:** **Through the steam** (< 0.45 s paper-cut whiteout). Emerge on the inflated/inflating raft hull with the concertina bellows attached.
- **24.0–30.5 s:** Narration 4 ("For inflation, they converted a musical concertina into a hand-pumped bellows, driving air through a single red valve.") Bellows pumps rhythmically; `6 × 14 FT` stencil holds.
- **30.5–32.5 s:** **Silence 2** (bellows stops, valve clicks shut—one heavy low stroke). Camera drops to water level.
- **32.5–39.5 s:** Narration 5 ("On the night of June eleventh, with hand-carved wooden paddles, they pushed off into San Francisco Bay.") Low glide over cut-ink waves; paddles dip; pilings rush past.
- **39.5–44.5 s:** Narration 6 ("The raft held. By morning, only the tide remained.") Camera rises and pulls back; the red valve and its red ripple shrink to a 3 px point.
- **44.5–48.0 s:** Final hold on the bay and distant Golden Gate/headland silhouette; `JUNE 11, 1962` stamped/carved; wind and bay swell fade.

## 6. Cue Map

- **Tempo:** 90 BPM (0.667 s per beat, 4/4 time, 2.667 s per bar). Total 18 bars = 48.0 s.
- **Section A — The Rock (Bars 1–3, 0.0–8.0 s):** Sparse pizzicato bass on beats 1 & 3; bowed vibraphone harmonic on bar 1 beat 1.
- **Section B — The Workshop & Steam (Bars 4–8, 8.0–21.33 s):** Dry woodblock/knife ticks on 8th notes; low pizzicato ostinato; **Silence** on Bar 8 beat 3–4 (20.67–22.0 s).
- **Section C — Steam Pass & Bellows (Bars 9–12, 21.33–32.0 s):** Steam hiss swells into Bar 9 beat 3 (23.33 s whiteout peak); rhythmic low horn + concertina-like reedy pulse (bowed vibraphone/horn) synced to bellows pumps at 25.0, 26.5, 28.0, 29.5 s; **Silence** at Bar 12 beat 3 (31.33 s), single timpani/low stroke on Bar 12 beat 4 (32.0 s).
- **Section D — The Bay & Vanishing (Bars 13–18, 32.0–48.0 s):** Slow 3-beat/4-beat horn and low strings swell; paddle strokes at 33.5, 35.5, 37.5 s; music thins out at Bar 16 (40.0 s), leaving only bay ambience for the last 4 seconds.
